Keyboard shortcuts

Alt Shift S
Start or stop recording; opens the panel
Alt Shift M
Switch between Full, Background and Mini
Esc
Close the open panel and go back where you were
Enter in Compose's address
Send

On a Mac, Option+Shift instead of Alt+Shift. Help → Keyboard shortcuts lists them in the panel; change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
